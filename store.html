Dolphin Singularity explores the strange, fascinating space between animal minds and human technology. This collection takes that curiosity off the page.

The original tee and hoodie carry the publication’s dolphin artwork. The graphic tees are for people who appreciate a clever animal, a questionable noise, and a joke that does not need a footnote. The slogans are jokes, not translations of dolphin calls.

Before you make a splash

The practical details.

Can I order now?

Yes. Choose a design and follow “Choose size on Printful” to select your size and order through the Dolphin Singularity Printful store. Shipping and taxes are calculated at checkout.

How will printing and shipping work?

The shop uses Printful Quick Stores. Customers order through Printful’s hosted store, with payment processed by Stripe. Printful makes each item to order and handles delivery. Production and delivery estimates are shown in the listing and checkout.

Where will the collection ship?

The launch is for US delivery addresses through Printful Quick Stores. International delivery is not part of this first launch.

How do I choose my size?

Use the size guide on the live product listing and compare its measurements with clothing you already own. Fit can differ between garment styles. The supplier mockups show the configured print; use the garment measurements to judge fit.

What about returns or a problem with an order?

Printful Quick Stores handles order support. Made-to-order items are not eligible for size, color, or change-of-mind exchanges. Its current policy covers qualifying damaged or defective items, with claims made within 30 days of delivery. Review the policy in the Printful shop before buying.
