Store & Support

Publication updates, reading resources, and ways to stay involved.

The Dolphin Singularity store is not open for orders. No merchandise launch date has been announced.

There is no checkout or merchandise waitlist on this website. If you arrived looking for a product shown on an older page, please contact Noah before making a purchase elsewhere. The links below lead to the publication and resources that are available now.

Follow the publication

Visit Dolphin Singularity on Substack to read the newsletter and see the current subscription options. Subscriptions are managed there, including payment, account settings, and cancellation. Read the plan description on Substack before choosing an option.

This is a subscription to the publication, not a reservation for future merchandise. We are not collecting separate store-launch registrations or promising launch discounts.

Subscribe on Substack

Explore without a purchase

The research archive, glossary, sound guide, and reading list are available to browse. Start with a topic that interests you, follow the source links, and use the newsletter for editorial context. The reading list links to books; this site does not sell or distribute those books.

Browse learning resources →

Support conservation directly

If you want to support field research or animal protection, the support page links to independent organizations. Review each organization’s work and donation terms on its own website. A publication subscription and a donation to an outside organization are separate choices.

Dolphin Singularity does not collect those donations or promise that a portion of a merchandise purchase will reach an organization.

Explore conservation support →

Ask about an idea

For a product suggestion, an educational resource request, or a proposed collaboration, use the contact page to email Noah. Include the page or item you are referring to and what you would like to know. Please do not send payment information by email.

Any future store will need clear product descriptions, pricing, delivery information, and purchase terms before orders are accepted. Until then, there is nothing to reserve here.
