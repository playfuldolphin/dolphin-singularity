Support Conservation

Choose a destination for your support.

Support the publication, explore conservation organizations, or start a conversation.

Dolphin Singularity does not currently process direct donations on this website. The options below take you to the relevant publication or organization, where you can review its work and any available ways to contribute.

Support Dolphin Singularity

For the newsletter, use the publication’s Substack subscription page. It shows the current plans and what each includes. Review those details there before subscribing. This route supports the publication and should not be confused with a donation to a separate conservation organization.

If you have an idea for a collaboration, a reporting project, or another kind of support, email Noah with a brief description. There is no payment to complete on this page.

Explore conservation and field research

Visit organizations directly to learn about their current projects, approach, and opportunities to contribute. Their own websites are the place to check payment details and the purpose of any appeal.

  • Dolphin Project
  • Wild Dolphin Project

These are external organizations. Following a link does not make a donation, and Dolphin Singularity does not collect payments on their behalf. Choose an organization based on the work you want to support and the information it publishes.

Contribute attention and knowledge

Support can also take the form of careful reading, sharing a useful source, or drawing attention to a correction. Begin with the research archive or the sound guide, and follow the references to their original context.

If you send a question about a conservation claim, include the page and the source you are comparing it with. That makes the question easier to investigate and gives us a clearer basis for improving the publication.
