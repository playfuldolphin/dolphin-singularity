Start with the work

Explore the newsletter page to see the publication’s focus and approach. The research archive, glossary, sound guide, and articles on this website are available to browse without creating an account here.

If you are deciding whether the publication is a good fit, start with a question that interests you. Follow an article to its sources and see whether the editorial perspective helps you understand the distinction between a result, an interpretation, and an open problem.

Choose on Substack

Use the subscription page to see the options currently offered by the publication. Review the price, billing schedule, and included access there before selecting a plan. Any subscription is completed on Substack; clicking the link on this page does not make a payment or create a subscription.

For an existing subscription, use your Substack account to review your plan and billing settings. If you need help reaching the right publication or have a question about its content, contact Noah at the email address below.

Other ways to contribute

A careful correction or a useful source can help the publication. When writing, include the article or page you are referring to and explain the specific point you would like checked. A link to the original paper is more useful than an isolated statistic.

You can also share a page with someone who would find it useful. If your interest is a research collaboration or an interview, describe the project and the kind of conversation you have in mind.
