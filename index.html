What this is

Marine cognition is advancing fast. The last five years have brought AI-powered acoustic analysis, large-scale population studies, and serious scientific attention to questions that were once considered fringe. Projects like DolphinGemma are applying transformer architectures to bottlenose dolphin vocalization data at a scale previously unimaginable. Project CETI is attempting the same with sperm whale codas. The tools are genuinely new. The results so far are genuinely mixed — and the popular coverage is almost uniformly worse than the research deserves.

This newsletter exists in the space between "dolphins have names" headlines and primary literature that assumes you have a background in acoustic signal processing. Each issue covers two or three recent developments — a new paper, a shift in methodology, a finding being overstated or undercovered. The voice has a point of view: honest about what's established, explicit about what's preliminary, willing to say when something is hype.
